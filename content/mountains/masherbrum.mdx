When the surveyor Thomas Montgomerie looked north from Kashmir in 1856, the first great peak he recorded in the Karakoram was Masherbrum. He labelled it **K1**. The next, hidden further away, became K2. Masherbrum kept its local name; K2 did not.

## The mountain

At 7,821 metres, Masherbrum stands between the Baltoro Glacier to the north and the Hushe valley to the south. Its north face, a great sweep of ice above the Baltoro, is one of the most admired sights from **Urdukas** on the trek to K2. Its southern side rises above the Masherbrum Glacier and the village of Hushe.

## History

Masherbrum was first climbed in **1960** by an American-Pakistani expedition, with **George Bell and Willi Unsoeld** reaching the summit.

## Seeing Masherbrum

- **On the Baltoro**, on the trek to [Concordia](/trekking/concordia) and [K2 Base Camp](/trekking/k2-base-camp).
