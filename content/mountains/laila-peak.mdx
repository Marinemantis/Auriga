Laila Peak rises above the Gondogoro Glacier in the **Hushe valley**. Seen from the meadows of Khuspang, it is an extraordinary shape: a slender, near-symmetrical spear of rock and ice, its summit a sharp point against the sky. Photographs of it travel the world.

## The mountain

At around 6,100 metres, Laila Peak is modest by Karakoram standards, but its shape makes it one of the most recognisable peaks in Pakistan. Its steep flanks are a serious challenge for climbers, and it has seen tragedy as well as success.

## Seeing Laila Peak

- **[Laila Peak Base Camp](/trekking/laila-peak-base-camp)**: the trek from Hushe up the Gondogoro valley to Khuspang, with the peak rising directly above camp.
- **[The Gondogoro La](/trekking/gondogoro-la)**: on the descent from the pass towards Hushe, Laila Peak dominates the view.

## Getting there

The Hushe valley is reached by road from Skardu via **[Khaplu](/destinations/skardu/khaplu)**, then by jeep to **[Hushe village](/trekking/hushe-valley)**.
