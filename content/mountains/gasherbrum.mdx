The Gasherbrums are a group of peaks around the Gasherbrum Glacier, at the eastern head of the Baltoro. The name is usually translated from Balti as the **shining mountain**. The group includes two of the world's fourteen 8,000-metre peaks.

## Gasherbrum I: Hidden Peak

At 8,080 metres, **Gasherbrum I** is the eleventh highest mountain in the world. It was named **Hidden Peak** by Martin Conway in 1892, because it stays out of sight for much of the approach. An American expedition made the first ascent in **1958**, when **Pete Schoening and Andy Kauffman** reached the summit.

## Gasherbrum II

At 8,035 metres, **Gasherbrum II** is the thirteenth highest mountain in the world, and considered one of the more accessible 8,000-metre peaks. An Austrian team made the first ascent in **1956**.

## Gasherbrum IV

The group also includes **Gasherbrum IV**, just under 8,000 metres, whose huge west face, the "Shining Wall", glows above Concordia at sunset. It is among the most famous sights of the Baltoro.

## Seeing the Gasherbrums

The Gasherbrums are seen on the trek up the Baltoro Glacier to **[Concordia](/trekking/concordia)**, and most memorably from the summit of the **[Gondogoro La](/trekking/gondogoro-la)**, which shows both Gasherbrum I and II alongside K2 and Broad Peak.
