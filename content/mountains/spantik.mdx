**Spantik**, at 7,027 metres, stands on the watershed between Nagar and Baltistan. It is known to most people as the **Golden Peak**, for the way its great north-west face, the **Golden Pillar**, glows gold at sunset. The face rises above the glaciers of Nagar and is one of the great challenges of Karakoram climbing.

## Climbing Spantik

Its normal route, from the Chogo Lungma Glacier on the Baltistan side, is considered one of the more accessible 7,000-metre climbs in Pakistan, and a popular first high peak for mountaineers. A German expedition made the first ascent in **1955**. The Golden Pillar, by contrast, is a serious technical climb, first climbed in 1987.
