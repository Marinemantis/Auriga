Broad Peak stands just south-east of K2, on the edge of the Godwin-Austen Glacier. Its long, broad summit ridge, with several summits, gave it its English name in 1892. In Balti it is called **Faichan Kangri**.

## The mountain

At 8,051 metres, Broad Peak is the twelfth highest mountain in the world. From **[Concordia](/trekking/concordia)** it looks almost gentle beside K2, a great wall of rock and snow with its summits strung along the top. It is one of the most frequently climbed 8,000-metre peaks in Pakistan, often attempted as preparation for K2.

## History

Broad Peak was first climbed in **1957** by a small Austrian team, **Hermann Buhl, Kurt Diemberger, Markus Schmuck and Fritz Wintersteller**, without supplementary oxygen or high-altitude porters, in a style far ahead of its time. Buhl, who had climbed Nanga Parbat alone in 1953, died weeks later on nearby Chogolisa.

## Seeing Broad Peak

The way to see Broad Peak is on foot, on the trek up the Baltoro Glacier to Concordia. Broad Peak Base Camp lies on the way to [K2 Base Camp](/trekking/k2-base-camp) and is often visited on the same day.
