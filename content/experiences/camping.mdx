There is no hotel on the Deosai Plains or beside Khukush Lake, and there should not be. The only way to stay is to camp, and to camp well.

## Our camps

- **Deosai Plains Camp**: canvas tents at 4,114 metres, with proper beds, gas heaters and one of the darkest skies in Pakistan. On [Where Mountains Meet the Stars](/tours/mountains-meet-stars).
- **Fairy Meadows Camp**: at the meadow edge, with Nanga Parbat filling the horizon at dawn. On [The Realm of Fairies](/tours/realm-of-fairies). See [camping at Fairy Meadows](/destinations/fairy-meadows/camping).
- **Khukush Alpine Camp**: expedition tents at the edge of a turquoise lake, for two nights. On [Khukush Lake & Phunder](/tours/khukush-lake-phunder).
- **Minimarg High Camp**: on the wildflower plateau near the Line of Control. On the [Seven Day Himalayan Dream](/tours/himalayan-dream).
- **Nagma Valley Camp**: deep in a hidden valley of Baltistan. On the [Nagma Valley Trek](/tours/nagma-valley).
- **Hoper Glacier Base Camp**: at the glacier edge in Nagar. On [Nagar Valley](/tours/nagar-valley).

## What to expect

Proper beds, warm bedding, gas heating where needed, a dining tent and a camp cook. Days end with dinner, a fire where allowed, and a sky full of stars; see our [stargazing guide](/experiences/stargazing).

## When to go

Most high camps run from around June to September, when the plateaus and meadows are open and green.
