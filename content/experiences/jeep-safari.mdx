In Gilgit-Baltistan, paved roads end long before the mountains do. Beyond them, jeep tracks climb into high valleys, over plateaus and along cliff edges to places that would otherwise take days to reach on foot.

## Great jeep journeys

- **The Deosai Plains**: across one of the highest plateaus on Earth, from Astore and Chilim to Skardu, past Sheosar Lake. See [Deosai](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains).
- **Raikot Bridge to Tato**: the famous narrow track towards [Fairy Meadows](/destinations/fairy-meadows/how-to-reach), high above the gorge.
- **Shandur Pass**: between Chitral and Ghizer, over the plateau of the world's highest polo ground.
- **Basho and Hushe**: into the green side valleys of Baltistan. See [Basho Valley](/destinations/skardu/basho-valley) and [Hushe](/trekking/hushe-valley).
- **Minimarg**: high into the meadows of Astore. See [Minimarg](/destinations/astore/minimarg).

## Safety and comfort

The driver is everything. We use private vehicles with experienced local drivers who know their routes in every season, and we never rush. Long days are broken with picnics, viewpoints and walks.
