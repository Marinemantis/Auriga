The Hunza valley is extraordinary from the ground. From the air, it is something else: terraced fields and orchards far below, the forts on their ridges, and the great peaks of the Karakoram all around.

## Tandem flights

You do not need any experience. On a tandem flight, a qualified pilot flies the wing and you are harnessed in front. After a short briefing, you run a few steps and the ground falls away. Flights typically last from a few minutes to much longer, depending on conditions.

## Where to fly

Hunza is the best-known place for paragliding in Gilgit-Baltistan, with launch sites on the slopes above the valley. Other valleys have hosted flights and events too.

## When to fly

Spring and autumn usually bring the most stable conditions. Flights need calm, clear weather, and pilots cancel when the wind is wrong. Morning is often best.

## Safety

Fly only with experienced, licensed pilots using proper, well-maintained equipment. We only arrange flights with operators we trust.
