Above the Baltoro Glacier, the granite spires of the **[Trango Towers](/pakistan/mountains/trango-towers)** rise in walls more than a kilometre high. They have drawn the world's best big-wall climbers since the first ascent of the Nameless Tower in 1976.

## What a Trango expedition involves

- **Permits**: climbing permits for the peak, and trekking permits for the Baltoro.
- **The approach**: a jeep journey from Skardu to Askole, then several days on foot up the Baltoro with porters.
- **Base camp**: on the glacier beneath the towers, for weeks at a time.
- **The climb**: multi-day big-wall routes on high-altitude granite.

## Who it is for

Experienced big-wall climbers only. The routes are long, high and committing, and weather can change quickly.

## For non-climbers

You do not need to climb to feel the towers' power. Trekkers pass beneath them on the way to [Concordia](/trekking/concordia) and [K2 Base Camp](/trekking/k2-base-camp).
