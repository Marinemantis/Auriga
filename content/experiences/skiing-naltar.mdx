Naltar is a valley of pine forest and alpine meadows above Gilgit. In summer it is known for its coloured lakes; in winter, it becomes one of Pakistan's ski areas.

## The ski area

Naltar has a ski slope with a lift among the pines, operated in connection with the Pakistan Air Force, and has hosted national and international competitions. It is small by Alpine standards, but the setting, with snow-covered peaks all around, is spectacular.

## The season

Skiing usually runs from around December to March, depending on snow. January and February are the most reliable months.

## Getting there

Naltar lies above Gilgit. The last stretch climbs a narrow mountain road, which in winter needs a suitable 4x4 and an experienced driver. See our [Naltar Valley guide](/destinations/gilgit/naltar-valley).

## What to know

- Facilities are limited. Check equipment hire, lift times and access before travelling.
- Bring proper winter clothing; see [Gilgit-Baltistan in winter](/travel/winter).
- Accommodation in the valley is basic and limited in winter.
