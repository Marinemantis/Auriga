The rivers of Gilgit-Baltistan are fed by some of the largest glaciers outside the polar regions. They are fast, cold, grey with glacial silt, and set in some of the most dramatic canyons on Earth.

## Where to raft

- **The Hunza River**, between the terraced villages of Hunza and Nagar.
- **The Gilgit River**, west of Gilgit towards Ghizer.
- **The Indus**, in sections around Skardu and the Gilgit area.

Which section suits depends on the season and on recent water levels.

## When to go

Spring and autumn usually bring the safest conditions. In high summer, snowmelt swells the rivers, and many sections become too powerful for a recreational trip.

## Safety

Glacier rivers are cold and powerful. Rafting should only be done with experienced guides, proper rafts, helmets, buoyancy aids and wetsuits, on sections chosen for the day's conditions. We only work with specialist operators.
