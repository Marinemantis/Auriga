Most trekkers to [K2 Base Camp](/trekking/k2-base-camp) return the way they came, a week back down the Baltoro Glacier. The Gondogoro La offers another way: a high pass south from Concordia over to the **Hushe valley**. It turns the trek into a loop, and its summit view is among the finest in the Himalaya and Karakoram.

## The crossing

From **[Concordia](/trekking/concordia)**, the route heads to Ali Camp at the foot of the pass. The climb usually begins around midnight, while the snow is firm and safe, on steep slopes secured with fixed ropes. Teams reach the top around dawn, as the sun rises on **K2, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum I and Gasherbrum II**, four of the world's 8,000-metre peaks, all in one panorama.

The descent on the Hushe side is steep, then easier, down to the Gondogoro Glacier, the meadows of **Khuspang** beneath **[Laila Peak](/pakistan/mountains/laila-peak)**, and on to Saicho and the village of **[Hushe](/trekking/hushe-valley)**.

## Difficulty and safety

The Gondogoro La is a serious undertaking. Conditions change with snowfall and temperature, and the pass is sometimes closed for safety. Crossings are made with experienced local guides, who fix the ropes each season, and crampons and a harness are needed. Good acclimatisation from the days on the Baltoro is essential.

## When to go

Usually from July to August, once the pass has been opened and fixed for the season.

## Permits

The route lies within a controlled area of the Central Karakoram National Park, and a trekking permit is required. See our guide to [NOCs and permits](/travel/noc-permits).
