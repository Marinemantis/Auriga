**[Spantik](/pakistan/mountains/spantik)**, at 7,027 metres, is a beautiful pyramid of rock and ice on the watershed between Nagar and Baltistan. Locally it is known as the **Golden Peak**, for the golden glow it radiates at sunset.

## Two approaches

Spantik can be approached from either side.

- **From Baltistan**, climbing expeditions usually set out from **Arandu**, at the head of the Basha valley, and walk up the Chogo Lungma Glacier to base camp.
- **From Nagar**, trekkers approach from the north through the valleys and glaciers above Nagar, where the mountain's **Golden Pillar**, its famous north-west face, rises above the ice.

## What the trek is like

The walk is a journey of slow revelation. River valleys give way to open moraine, and the peak emerges dramatically above a glacier-carved landscape. Days are spent on rough ground at altitude, with nights in wilderness camps.

## On our journeys

On our [Nagar Valley](/tours/nagar-valley) journey, after the [Rakaposhi Base Camp trek](/trekking/rakaposhi-base-camp), we push deeper into the Karakoram towards the base camp of Golden Peak, before ending with the Hoper Glacier and Michar Kho Valley. Nights are spent in hand-selected lodges and our private wilderness camps, with proper beds and a camp cook.

## When to go

From June to September, when the high ground is clear of snow.
