Between Gilgit and Skardu, the road follows the Indus through a deep, dramatic gorge. High above it, hidden from the road, lies the Haramosh Valley, and at its head the green waters of **Kutwal Lake**, beneath the glaciers of **[Haramosh Peak](/pakistan/mountains/haramosh)** (7,409 m).

## The route

The trek begins near **Sassi** on the Gilgit–Skardu road. A jeep track and then a steep trail climb to the village of **Dassu**, and on through **Iskere** into the valley. The route continues through forest and fields to **Kutwal village** and the lake, at around 3,300 metres, with the Mani Glacier and the walls of Haramosh above.

From Kutwal, strong trekkers can continue towards Mani Glacier and the high pastures beneath the mountain.

## The valley

Haramosh is a quiet valley of small villages, pine and birch forest, and summer pastures. It sees far fewer trekkers than the Baltoro or Fairy Meadows, and it has the feel of somewhere still undiscovered.

## When to go

From June to September, when the trail and the high pastures are clear of snow.

## Difficulty

Moderate. The first climb from the Indus is steep and hot in summer, so we start early. After that, the walking is steady, and the altitude stays modest.
