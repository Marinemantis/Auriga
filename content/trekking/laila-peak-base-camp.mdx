**[Laila Peak](/pakistan/mountains/laila-peak)** is one of the most photographed mountains in the Karakoram: a slender, impossibly sharp spear of rock and ice above the Gondogoro Glacier. The trek to its base camp from **Hushe** is one of the most rewarding shorter treks in Baltistan.

## The route

From **[Hushe village](/trekking/hushe-valley)**, at the head of the Hushe valley, the trail heads north to **Saicho**, a camp among trees at the meeting of valleys. From there it climbs up the side of the **Gondogoro Glacier** to the summer pastures of **Dalsampa** and **Khuspang**, where Laila Peak rises directly above camp. Masherbrum and other great peaks are close by.

Many trekkers spend an extra day at Khuspang to walk higher for views, before returning the same way.

## Difficulty

Moderate to strenuous. The walking is not technical, but there is moraine to cross and the altitude reaches around 4,500 metres or more, so acclimatisation matters. It is also the approach to the **[Gondogoro La](/trekking/gondogoro-la)**, for those crossing to Concordia in reverse.

## When to go

From June to September. July and August are the most settled months.
