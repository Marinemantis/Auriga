K2, at 8,611 metres, is the second highest mountain in the world and, to many climbers, the most beautiful. Reaching its base camp means walking for a week or more up the Baltoro Glacier, through a gallery of giants: the Trango Towers, Masherbrum, Broad Peak and the Gasherbrums, until the glacier meets others at Concordia and K2 fills the head of the valley.

## The route

The trek begins in **Skardu**. A long jeep drive up the Shigar and Braldu valleys reaches **Askole**, the last village. From there, the trail follows the Braldu River to the snout of the Baltoro Glacier at **Paiju**, then climbs the glacier itself, camp by camp, past **Urdukas** and **Goro**, to **[Concordia](/trekking/concordia)** at around 4,600 metres. A final day or two on the Godwin-Austen Glacier leads to K2 Base Camp, at around 5,000 metres.

Most trekkers return the same way. The more adventurous cross the **[Gondogoro La](/trekking/gondogoro-la)**, a high pass, and descend to the Hushe valley.

## Itinerary outline

A typical outline from Skardu:

| Days | Stage |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Skardu: arrival, preparation, permits |
| 3 | Jeep to Askole |
| 4–6 | Askole to Jhola and Paiju; rest day at Paiju |
| 7–9 | Up the Baltoro: Urdukas and Goro |
| 10 | Goro to Concordia |
| 11–12 | K2 Base Camp and back to Concordia |
| 13–17 | Return down the Baltoro to Askole, or cross the Gondogoro La to Hushe |
| 18 | Drive to Skardu |

Exact stages vary with pace, weather and acclimatisation. We build in spare days.

## Permits

The Baltoro lies in a controlled area within the Central Karakoram National Park. Trekkers need a trekking permit and must pay park fees, and the trek must be organised through a registered operator. Requirements change, so see our guide to [NOCs and permits](/travel/noc-permits); we arrange everything for our guests.

## Best time to go

From **mid-June to August**. July is the most popular month, when climbing expeditions are on K2 and Broad Peak. Earlier in June there can still be deep snow on the upper glacier; by September the weather becomes less settled.

## Cost

The cost depends on group size, comfort level, the route and the support you want: porters, cooks, guides, camp equipment and permits all add up. We plan every K2 trek as a tailor made journey, so [ask us for a quote](/enquire).

## How to prepare

- Train for long days on rough ground, carrying a daypack, for several months beforehand.
- Prior experience of trekking above 4,000 metres is strongly recommended.
- Allow time for acclimatisation, and never rush the ascent.
- Bring proper high-altitude equipment. We send guests a full kit list.
