Concordia is the point where the Baltoro Glacier meets the Godwin-Austen Glacier and several others in a vast, flat amphitheatre of ice and rock. The name came from early European explorers, after the Place de la Concorde in Paris. The mountaineer and photographer Galen Rowell called it "the throne room of the mountain gods", and once you stand there it is hard to argue.

## The view

From Concordia, **[K2](/pakistan/mountains/k2)** rises alone at the head of the Godwin-Austen Glacier, a perfect pyramid of rock and ice. To the east stand **[Broad Peak](/pakistan/mountains/broad-peak)** and the **[Gasherbrums](/pakistan/mountains/gasherbrum)**. Around them are Mitre Peak, Chogolisa and many other summits above 6,000 and 7,000 metres. At dawn and dusk, the light moves across the peaks one by one.

## Getting there

Concordia is reached on foot from **Askole**, the last village in the Braldu valley, up the Baltoro Glacier via Paiju, Urdukas and Goro. It takes around a week, with a rest day. See our full [K2 Base Camp trek guide](/trekking/k2-base-camp) for the route and itinerary.

## Beyond Concordia

Most trekkers spend at least one extra day here, walking up to **K2 Base Camp** and back, or to Broad Peak Base Camp. The adventurous then cross the **[Gondogoro La](/trekking/gondogoro-la)** to Hushe, rather than returning down the Baltoro.

## When to go

From mid-June to August, when the glacier route is clear and the weather is most settled.

## Permits and planning
