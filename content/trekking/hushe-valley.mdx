The Hushe valley branches north from the Shyok River near **[Khaplu](/destinations/skardu/khaplu)**. A road climbs through a string of villages to **Hushe**, the last settlement, at around 3,000 metres, with the great south face of Masherbrum at the head of the valley.

## The village

Hushe is a small Balti village of stone houses, barley fields and apricot trees. Many families have worked for generations as high-altitude porters and guides, and the village has a long, proud connection with mountaineering. It is a warm and welcoming place to spend a night before or after a trek.

## Treks from Hushe

- **[Masherbrum Base Camp](/trekking/masherbrum-base-camp)**, up the Masherbrum Glacier beneath K1.
- **[Laila Peak Base Camp](/trekking/laila-peak-base-camp)**, up the Gondogoro valley to Khuspang.
- **[The Gondogoro La](/trekking/gondogoro-la)**, the high pass to Concordia and the Baltoro.
- **The Charakusa valley**, beneath the granite walls of K6 and K7, a magnet for climbers.

## Getting there

From Skardu, the road follows the Indus and Shyok east to Khaplu, around two and a half to three hours. From Khaplu, a jeep road climbs the Hushe valley, around three hours more.

## When to go

From June to September for trekking. Spring and autumn are beautiful in the lower valley, but the high routes are snowbound.
