**Karambar Lake** lies on the Karambar Pass, at around 4,300 metres, on the watershed between the **Broghil** valley in northern Chitral and the **Ishkoman** valley in Ghizer. To the north lies Afghanistan's Wakhan Corridor. It is a wide, wind-swept landscape of lakes, grassland and glaciers, grazed in summer by the yaks of Wakhi herders.

## Approaches

There are two ways in.

- **From Chitral**, a long jeep journey reaches the Broghil valley, a landscape of high lakes and pastures. From the end of the road, the trek to Karambar Lake takes several days.
- **From Ghizer**, the route follows the Ishkoman valley north from the village of Imit, past the Karambar River and glaciers, to the pass and the lake. It is longer and more demanding.

Strong trekkers can cross from one side to the other, linking Chitral and Ghizer over the pass.

## Difficulty

Strenuous, mainly because of the remoteness and altitude rather than technical terrain. Acclimatisation is essential, and good support is needed this far from roads.

## When to go

July to September, when the passes and pastures are open. Even in summer, nights are cold.

## Permits

Broghil and Karambar lie close to the border, and rules for foreign visitors change. See our guide to [NOCs and permits](/travel/noc-permits).
