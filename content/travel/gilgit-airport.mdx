Gilgit Airport lies close to the city, in a deep valley surrounded by mountains. It is the gateway to Hunza, Nagar, Fairy Meadows, Astore and Ghizer, and most of our journeys begin here.

## Flights

The main route is **Islamabad to Gilgit**, around an hour. The aircraft flies through the mountains, past the great flank of **Nanga Parbat**, and the approach into the Gilgit valley is dramatic.

## Weather

Flights depend on clear weather and good visibility along the route. Delays and cancellations are common, especially outside summer. We always book the earliest flights of the day, build in a spare day, and keep a road plan ready.

## Onward from Gilgit

- **Hunza**: around two to three hours up the Karakoram Highway to Karimabad.
- **[Fairy Meadows](/destinations/fairy-meadows/how-to-reach)**: around two hours to Raikot Bridge, then jeep and walk.
- **Astore**: a few hours south, towards Rama Lake and the Rupal Valley.
- **Ghizer**: west along the Gilgit River to Gupis and Phander.

## Tips

- Keep a warm layer, medicines and valuables in your hand luggage.
- Check baggage limits, which can be strict on these routes.
- Keep plans flexible: the mountains set the timetable.
