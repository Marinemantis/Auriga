Skardu, the capital of Baltistan, lies around 600 to 700 kilometres from Islamabad by road, beyond some of the highest mountains on Earth. You can fly in about an hour, or take two days by road.

## Flying

The flight from Islamabad to Skardu is one of the great flights of the world. On a clear morning, the aircraft passes Nanga Parbat close enough to see its glaciers, and the peaks of the Karakoram line the horizon. See our guide to [Skardu flights and the airport](/travel/skardu-flights).

Like all flights to the north, it needs clear weather. Delays and cancellations are common, so we always build in a spare day.

## By road

The road route follows the Karakoram Highway north through the Indus gorge to the area around Gilgit, then turns east along the **[Gilgit–Skardu road](/travel/gilgit-to-skardu)**, following the Indus to Skardu. Most travellers break the journey for a night.

## Via Deosai (summer)

In summer, you can also travel from Astore across the **[Deosai Plains](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains)** into Skardu, a slow, high, unforgettable route usually open from around June to September. It is how our [Where Mountains Meet the Stars](/tours/mountains-meet-stars) journey reaches Skardu.

## What we recommend

Fly in if the weather allows, and leave by road, or the other way round. With time, cross Deosai in summer: it is one of the great journeys of the north.
