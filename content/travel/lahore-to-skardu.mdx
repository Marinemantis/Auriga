Skardu is one of the most remote cities in Pakistan, and Lahore one of the furthest from it. The journey can take an hour or three days, depending on how you travel and the weather.

## By air

The quickest way is to fly. Most travellers connect through **Islamabad**, from where the flight to Skardu takes around an hour and passes close to Nanga Parbat. Direct flights from Lahore have operated at times; check when you book. See our guide to [Skardu flights and the airport](/travel/skardu-flights).

Flights to Skardu need clear weather, and delays are common, so always allow a spare day.

## By road

1. **Lahore to Islamabad** on the M2 motorway, around four to five hours.
2. **Islamabad to the Gilgit area** along the Karakoram Highway, usually with a night on the way.
3. **On to Skardu** along the Indus. See our [Gilgit to Skardu guide](/travel/gilgit-to-skardu).

In summer, you can instead cross the Babusar Pass to the Karakoram Highway, or travel via Astore and the Deosai Plains into Skardu.

## What we recommend

Fly one way and drive the other, with a spare day for weather. If you travel by road, never rush it: the mountain roads are slow, and the scenery deserves daylight.
