Gilgit and Skardu are the two great towns of Gilgit-Baltistan, and the road between them is one of the most dramatic in Pakistan.

## The route

From Gilgit, the road heads south to the confluence of the Gilgit and Indus rivers, near the point where the Himalaya, Karakoram and Hindu Kush meet. It then turns east and follows the **Indus** upstream through a deep, narrow gorge, past Sassi, the mouth of the **[Haramosh Valley](/destinations/gilgit/haramosh-valley)**, and the region of Rondu, before the valley opens out into the wide basin of Skardu.

## How long it takes

Allow around four to six hours, depending on road conditions and roadworks. Landslides can close the road for hours in spring and during the monsoon, so we always plan with time to spare.

## The Deosai route (summer)

In summer there is a second route: south from Gilgit to **Astore**, then across the **[Deosai Plains](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains)** and down past Satpara Lake into Skardu. It is slower and much higher, crossing a plateau at around 4,000 metres, and it is one of the great journeys of the north.
