From December to February, winter settles over Gilgit-Baltistan. Nights fall well below freezing across the region, and far lower in the high valleys. Days are often clear and bright, with snow on every peak.

## What stays open

- **Hunza**, **Gilgit** and **Skardu** remain accessible, with some hotels open all winter.
- The **Karakoram Highway** stays open to Upper Hunza, though snow and rockfall can cause delays.
- **Flights** to Gilgit and Skardu continue, but are more often delayed or cancelled by weather.

## What closes

- The **Khunjerab**, **Babusar** and **Shandur** passes.
- The **Deosai Plains**, and most high treks and camps.
- **Many hotels and guesthouses**, especially in smaller valleys.

## Where to go

- **Hunza**: snow on the villages, frozen streams, and the forts to yourself. See [Hunza in winter](/destinations/hunza/in-winter).
- **Skardu**: crisp, dry cold, and the lakes and deserts in winter light. See [Skardu weather](/destinations/skardu/weather).
- **Naltar**: [skiing](/experiences/skiing-naltar) among the pines above Gilgit.

## Planning a winter journey

Choose hotels that stay open and are properly heated. Build spare days into every plan. Bring proper winter clothing; see [what to pack](/travel/what-to-pack). And travel with people who know how the north works in winter.
