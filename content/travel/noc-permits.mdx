Questions about NOCs ("No Objection Certificates") and permits come up on almost every call we have about northern Pakistan. The short answer is reassuring: for most journeys through the main valleys, you need nothing beyond the right visa. The longer answer depends on exactly where you are going.

## Main tourist areas

Gilgit, Hunza, Nagar, Skardu, Shigar, Khaplu, Fairy Meadows and the main roads between them are open to visitors. Foreign travellers need a valid [Pakistani visa](/pakistan/visa) and should carry their passport at all times, as there are routine checkpoints along the Karakoram Highway and other main roads where details are recorded. Domestic travellers carry their CNIC.

## Restricted areas near the borders

Some areas close to Pakistan's borders and the Line of Control are restricted, and visiting them can require prior permission from the authorities. Depending on the current rules, this can include places such as Minimarg in Astore, parts of the upper valleys near the Afghan and Chinese borders, and other border zones. Requirements differ for foreign and Pakistani nationals and can change at short notice.

## Trekking permits

Many treks in Gilgit-Baltistan need no special permit. Others fall within designated restricted zones, which include much of the Baltoro region around K2 and Concordia. Treks there require a trekking permit, which is arranged through a licensed Pakistani tour operator, and the trekking party may need to be accompanied by an officially appointed guide or liaison officer.

## Climbing permits

Climbing expeditions on major peaks need a mountaineering permit and royalty, arranged through a licensed operator well in advance of the season.

## National parks and local fees

Some protected areas, such as Deosai National Park, charge an entry fee at the park gates, and some villages and heritage sites charge a small fee for visitors.

## How we handle it

For every journey we run, we check the current requirements for each place on the itinerary, arrange any permits needed, and tell you in advance exactly what to carry. If your route includes a restricted area, we apply for permission early so it is in place before you travel.
