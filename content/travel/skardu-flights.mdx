Skardu has the region's main airport, and the flight in is one of the great short flights of the world.

## Routes

The main route is **Islamabad to Skardu**, around an hour. Flights from other cities, including Lahore and Karachi, have operated at times; schedules change, so check when you book.

## Weather

Flights to Skardu depend on clear weather and good visibility along the route. Cloud, rain or strong wind can mean delays or cancellations, especially in spring, autumn and winter. Summer is most reliable, but nothing is guaranteed.

That is why we always:

- book the earliest flights of the day, when weather is often clearest;
- build a spare day into every journey;
- have a road plan ready if flights are cancelled.

## The views

On a clear day, the aircraft passes close to **Nanga Parbat**, and the peaks of the Karakoram, sometimes including K2, line the horizon. The descent into the wide Skardu valley, between the Indus and the sand dunes, is unforgettable.

## Skardu Airport

The airport lies a short drive from Skardu town, beside the [Katpana cold desert](/destinations/skardu/katpana-desert). It is small and simple. Allow time for security and baggage, and bring a warm layer: the air is cooler than in Islamabad.

## Tips

- Keep valuables, medicines and a warm layer in your hand luggage.
- Check baggage limits, which can be stricter than on international flights.
- Expect changes, and keep plans flexible.
