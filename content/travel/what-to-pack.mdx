Northern Pakistan rewards travellers who pack sensibly. Altitude changes everything: a warm afternoon in Skardu can become a freezing night on the Deosai Plains, and the sun is fierce at any height.

## Clothing: think in layers

- **Base layers**: breathable T-shirts and long-sleeved tops.
- **Mid layer**: a fleece or light insulated jacket.
- **Outer layer**: a windproof, waterproof jacket.
- **Warm extras**: a down jacket for high places and evenings, a warm hat and gloves.
- **Trousers**: comfortable, quick-drying trousers. Shorts are not usual in the north.

## Dressing respectfully

Gilgit-Baltistan is conservative. Long trousers or skirts and tops covering the shoulders are appropriate for everyone. Women may want a light scarf for visiting villages, homes and mosques.

## Footwear

Broken-in walking boots for treks and hill walks, comfortable trainers for travel days, and sandals for hotels and camps.

## Sun and health

- High-factor sunscreen, lip balm and good sunglasses.
- A refillable water bottle.
- Personal medicines, plus basics for stomach upsets and headaches.
- If you are trekking high, talk to your doctor about altitude.

## Documents and money

Your passport, visa and copies (see [Pakistan visa help](/pakistan/visa)), travel insurance details, and some cash: card machines are rare outside the main towns. Permits, where needed, are covered in our [NOC and permits guide](/travel/noc-permits).

## For camping nights

On journeys with a night at camp, we provide tents, beds and bedding. Bring a head torch, warm sleepwear and a power bank.

## Luggage

A soft bag travels better than a hard case on mountain roads and small aircraft, and baggage limits on domestic flights can be strict.
