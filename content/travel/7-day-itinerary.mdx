The most common mistake on a short trip to the north is trying to see everything. Distances are long and mountain roads are slow. In seven days, choose one region and enjoy it properly.

## Option 1: Hunza and the Karakoram Highway

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fly Islamabad to Gilgit; drive to Karimabad |
| 2 | [Baltit](/destinations/hunza/baltit-fort) and [Altit](/destinations/hunza/altit-fort) forts; water channel walk |
| 3 | Sunrise at [Eagle's Nest](/destinations/hunza/eagles-nest); afternoon in Karimabad |
| 4 | [Attabad Lake](/destinations/hunza/attabad-lake), [Gulmit](/destinations/hunza/gulmit), Hussaini bridge, [Passu Cones](/destinations/hunza/passu-cones) |
| 5 | [Khunjerab Pass](/destinations/hunza/khunjerab-pass) (in season) |
| 6 | Return to Gilgit |
| 7 | Fly to Islamabad (spare day for weather) |

## Option 2: Astore and Nanga Parbat

Our [Seven Day Himalayan Dream](/tours/himalayan-dream) begins and ends in Gilgit, with [Rama Lake](/destinations/astore/rama-lake), the [Rupal Face](/trekking/rupal-face) of Nanga Parbat, and a night at [Minimarg](/destinations/astore/minimarg).

## Option 3: Ghizer's lakes

Our [Khukush Lake & Phunder](/tours/khukush-lake-phunder) journey travels from Gilgit to Khalti Lake and [Phander](/destinations/ghizer/phander-lake), then on foot to two nights in a luxury camp at Khukush Lake.

## Tips for a 7-day trip

- Fly to Gilgit if possible, and keep one day spare for weather.
- Choose one region, and resist adding a second.
- Travel in the shoulder seasons, May–June or September–October, for the best balance of weather and quiet.
