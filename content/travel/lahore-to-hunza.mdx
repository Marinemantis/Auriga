Lahore and Hunza are far apart: the journey runs from the plains of Punjab to the high Karakoram. Almost every route passes through **Islamabad**.

## Step one: Lahore to Islamabad

The **M2 motorway** links Lahore and Islamabad, a smooth drive of around four to five hours. You can also fly, though by the time you reach the airport the motorway is often almost as quick.

## Step two: Islamabad to Hunza

From Islamabad, you have the same choices as any traveller heading north:

- **Fly to Gilgit**, around an hour, then drive two to three hours to Karimabad. Weather dependent.
- **Drive the [Karakoram Highway](/travel/karakoram-highway)**, usually over two days.
- **In summer, cross the Babusar Pass** through the Kaghan Valley, a shorter, higher route.

See our full guide to [Islamabad to Hunza](/travel/islamabad-to-hunza).

## Breaking the journey

Driving all the way from Lahore, plan at least one night in Islamabad and one on the road north. Rushing is the most common mistake. The roads are long, mountain driving is slow, and the scenery deserves daylight.

## What we recommend

Spend a night in Islamabad, then fly to Gilgit if the weather is good, with a spare day built in. Return by road along the Karakoram Highway to see the mountains at ground level.
