Hunza lies around 600 kilometres north of Islamabad by road, deep in the Karakoram. Getting there is part of the adventure.

## Flying: Islamabad to Gilgit

The quickest way is a flight from Islamabad to **Gilgit**, around an hour, past the great wall of Nanga Parbat. From [Gilgit Airport](/travel/gilgit-airport), it is a drive of around two to three hours up the Karakoram Highway to Karimabad.

Flights depend on clear weather, because pilots fly by sight through the mountains. Delays and cancellations are common, so we always build a spare day into the plan.

## By road: the Karakoram Highway

The classic route follows the motorway north to Hazara, then the **[Karakoram Highway](/travel/karakoram-highway)** through Besham, Dasu and Chilas, along the Indus gorge to Gilgit and Hunza. It is a long drive, usually broken with a night on the way, with extraordinary scenery all along the Indus.

## By road: the Babusar Pass (summer)

In summer, a shorter and higher route leaves the plains through the **Kaghan Valley** and Naran, crosses the **Babusar Pass**, and drops to the Karakoram Highway near Chilas. The pass is usually open only from around June to October, depending on snow.

## What we recommend

Fly one way and drive the other. You save time, see the mountains from the air, and experience the Karakoram Highway at ground level. We arrange flights, private vehicles, experienced drivers and comfortable stops, and we plan for the weather.
