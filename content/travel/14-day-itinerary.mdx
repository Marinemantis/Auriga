With two weeks, you can combine two regions of the north without rushing. Here are the two routes we think work best.

## Option 1: Skardu and Hunza

The classic combination. Our [The Last Paradise](/tours/last-paradise) journey (12 days) follows this route.

| Days | Plan |
|---|---|
| 1 | Arrive in Islamabad |
| 2 | Fly to Skardu |
| 3–5 | [Shigar Fort](/destinations/skardu/shigar-fort), the [Kachura lakes](/destinations/skardu/kachura-lake), [Katpana desert](/destinations/skardu/katpana-desert), [Khaplu](/destinations/skardu/khaplu) |
| 6 | Summer: [Deosai](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains). Otherwise a free day |
| 7 | [Skardu to Gilgit](/travel/gilgit-to-skardu) along the Indus |
| 8–11 | Hunza: the forts, [Eagle's Nest](/destinations/hunza/eagles-nest), Upper Hunza, [Khunjerab](/destinations/hunza/khunjerab-pass) |
| 12 | Return to Gilgit |
| 13 | Fly to Islamabad |
| 14 | Spare day for weather; depart |

## Option 2: Chitral, Shandur and Ghizer

For travellers who want the less-travelled north. Our [Wilderness of Chitral & Phunder](/tours/chitral-phunder) journey (12 nights) begins in [Chitral](/destinations/chitral) with the Kalash valleys, crosses the [Shandur Pass](/pakistan/festivals/shandur-polo-festival), and descends through [Phander](/destinations/ghizer/phander-lake), [Yasin](/destinations/ghizer/yasin-valley) and [Gupis](/destinations/ghizer/gupis) to Gilgit.

## Adding Fairy Meadows

With two weeks in Hunza and the Gilgit region, add two nights at [Fairy Meadows](/destinations/fairy-meadows) beneath Nanga Parbat, as on [The Realm of Fairies](/tours/realm-of-fairies).

## Tips

- Keep at least one spare day for weather.
- Fly one way and drive the other.
- Don't add a third region. Use the extra time to slow down.
