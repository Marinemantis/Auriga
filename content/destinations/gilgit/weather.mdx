Gilgit sits low for Gilgit-Baltistan, at around 1,500 metres, in a deep valley surrounded by much higher mountains. That shapes its weather in two ways. It is far hotter in summer than the valleys around it, and it is extraordinarily dry: the mountains block most of the monsoon, so the city gets less rain in a year than many places get in a month.

## Spring: March to May

Spring is one of the best times to be in Gilgit. Days warm quickly from the mid-teens in March to the high twenties by May, and the apricot and cherry orchards in the surrounding valleys come into blossom. It is also the "wet" season, although wet is relative: April and May each see only around 25 mm of rain, usually in short showers.

## Summer: June to August

Gilgit is hot in summer, with average highs of 34–36°C and occasional days well above 40°C. Most travellers pass through rather than linger, heading up into Hunza, Naltar or Fairy Meadows, where the air is far cooler. Summer is when the high passes and treks are open, so it is the busiest season in the mountains around Gilgit.

## Autumn: September to November

September and October bring warm, clear days, cooler nights and very little rain, and the poplars in the valleys turn gold through October. By November the nights fall to around freezing. For many travellers, autumn is the most rewarding time of year in the region.

## Winter: December to February

Winter in Gilgit is cold but bright. Daytime highs reach around 10–12°C, nights drop below freezing, and the city is usually snow-free. The surrounding mountains are a different matter, and higher roads and passes can close, so winter journeys need more flexibility.

## Weather in the valleys around Gilgit

Gilgit's figures are a poor guide to the places most travellers are heading. Hunza, Naltar and Fairy Meadows are 1,000 to 2,000 metres higher, and temperatures fall by roughly six or seven degrees with every thousand metres you climb. When Gilgit is 35°C, Fairy Meadows can be pleasantly cool and the nights cold. See our [Hunza weather guide](/destinations/hunza/weather) for the valley to the north.
