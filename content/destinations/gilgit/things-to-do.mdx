Gilgit sits at around 1,500 metres in a wide valley where the Gilgit and Hunza rivers meet. It is the capital of Gilgit-Baltistan and the crossroads of the north: every road to Hunza, Astore, Ghizer and Skardu passes nearby.

## In and around the city

- **[The Kargah Buddha](/destinations/gilgit/kargah-buddha)**: a standing Buddha carved into a cliff west of the city, more than a thousand years old.
- **The bazaar**: busy, colourful and full of dried fruit, gemstones, shawls and goods from China.
- **The Danyor suspension bridge** across the river, and the nearby **Chinese cemetery**, remembering workers who died building the Karakoram Highway.
- **Polo**: Gilgit is a home of mountain polo, and matches are played in the city in season.

## The meeting of three ranges

South of Gilgit, near Jaglot, a viewpoint marks the meeting point of the **Himalaya, the Karakoram and the Hindu Kush**, one of the only places on Earth where three great ranges meet.

## Day trips

- **[Naltar Valley](/destinations/gilgit/naltar-valley)**: pine forests and coloured lakes above the city.
- **[Bagrot Valley](/destinations/gilgit/bagrot-valley)**: a quiet valley beneath the south side of Rakaposhi and Diran.
- **Khalti Lake**, on the road west to Ghizer.
- **[Haramosh Valley](/destinations/gilgit/haramosh-valley)**, above the Indus towards Skardu.

## When to go

Gilgit is very hot in midsummer. Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons. See our [Gilgit weather guide](/destinations/gilgit/weather).
