The road from [Gilgit to Skardu](/travel/gilgit-to-skardu) follows the Indus through a deep gorge. Above it, out of sight, lies the Haramosh Valley: small villages, pine and birch forest, summer pastures, and at its head the walls of **[Haramosh Peak](/pakistan/mountains/haramosh)**, at 7,409 metres.

## The valley

Haramosh is a quiet, traditional valley. Villages such as **Dassu**, **Iskere** and **Kutwal** sit on the slopes, reached only on foot or by rough track. Few visitors come, and that is its charm.

## Kutwal Lake

At the head of the valley, **Kutwal Lake** lies in a green bowl beneath the glaciers of Haramosh. Reaching it is a trek of several days from the road. See our full guide to the [Kutwal Lake trek](/trekking/kutwal-lake).

## Getting there

The valley starts near **Sassi** on the Gilgit–Skardu road. From there, a steep climb leads up into the valley.

## When to go

June to September, when the trails and pastures are clear of snow.
