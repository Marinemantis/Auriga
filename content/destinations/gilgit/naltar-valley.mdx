Naltar is one of the most beautiful valleys near Gilgit: a place of pine forests, wide meadows and clear streams, with snow peaks above. After the heat and bustle of the city, it feels like another world.

## The lakes

The upper valley holds a series of small lakes, known for their vivid colours, from turquoise to deep green. The best known include **Satrangi Lake** and **Blue Lake**. See our guide to the [Naltar lakes](/trekking/naltar-lakes).

## The forests and meadows

Naltar's pine forests are among the finest in the region, and the meadows of the upper valley are perfect for walks and picnics. Villages in the lower valley grow potatoes and fruit in the short summer.

## Winter: skiing

In winter, snow covers the valley and Naltar becomes one of Pakistan's ski areas. See [skiing in Naltar](/experiences/skiing-naltar).

## Getting there

From Gilgit, a road climbs into the valley. The upper section to the lakes is rough and best tackled in a 4x4 with an experienced driver.

## When to go

June to October for the lakes and meadows. Autumn brings gold to the forests. December to March for snow and skiing.
