Most visitors see Rakaposhi from the north, from Hunza and Nagar. Bagrot shows its other side. The valley runs up from near Gilgit towards the southern walls of **[Rakaposhi](/pakistan/mountains/rakaposhi)** and **Diran**, through a string of quiet villages, terraced fields and orchards.

## The valley

Bagrot is a farming valley, little changed by tourism. Villages sit among walnut and apricot trees, and water channels carry glacier melt to the fields. At the head of the valley, glaciers spill down from the peaks.

## Walks

From the upper villages, paths lead towards the glaciers and summer pastures, with close views of Diran and Rakaposhi. Short walks suit most travellers; longer routes continue towards the high pastures.

## Getting there

Bagrot is reached by road from the Gilgit area, with the upper section on a rough jeep track. It makes a good day trip from Gilgit.

## When to go

April to October. In spring, the orchards blossom; in autumn, they turn gold.
