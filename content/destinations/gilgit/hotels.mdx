Gilgit is the gateway to the north, and for most travellers it is a place to rest at the start or end of a journey: after a long drive up the Karakoram Highway, or before the flight back to Islamabad.

## What to look for

- **Quiet**: the city is busy, so a room away from the main roads matters.
- **A garden**: Gilgit's best hotels have green gardens, a welcome escape in summer.
- **Cooling**: summer afternoons are hot, often above 35°C. See [Gilgit weather](/destinations/gilgit/weather).
- **Reliable power and good food**.

## Where we stay

If your flight lands early, you can often continue to Hunza the same day, a drive of two to three hours. See [Hunza hotels](/destinations/hunza/hotels).
