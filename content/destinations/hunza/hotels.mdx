Hunza is a long valley, and the places to stay are spread along it, from the busy centre around Karimabad to the quiet villages of Upper Hunza near the Chinese border. Choosing the right base matters more here than in most places, because the valley's best moments, sunrise over Rakaposhi and evening light on the Passu Cones, happen at the edges of the day.

## Karimabad: the heart of the valley

Karimabad is where most travellers stay, and for good reason. It sits beneath Baltit Fort, with Altit Fort a short drive away, and its lanes, cafés and craft shops are easy to explore on foot. The views across to Rakaposhi and the peaks of Nagar are some of the finest in the valley.

**Where we stay:** Serena Hunza, perched above Karimabad with uninterrupted views of Rakaposhi and traditional Hunzai architecture.

## Duikar: for the view

High above Altit, the village of Duikar is home to the [Eagle's Nest viewpoint](/destinations/hunza/eagles-nest), famous for sunrise and sunset over the peaks of central Hunza. Staying here means being on the ridge at first light without a pre-dawn drive.

**Where we stay:** the Eagle's Nest Hotel, with private cottages above the valley and sunset views across the surrounding ranges.

## Attabad Lake

Along the shores of [Attabad Lake](/destinations/hunza/attabad-lake), a handful of places to stay look out over the turquoise water. It is a good halfway base between central and Upper Hunza.

## Upper Hunza (Gojal)

North of the lake, the villages of Gulmit, Hussaini and Passu are quieter and more traditional, with simpler guesthouses and some of the most dramatic scenery in the valley, including the [Passu Cones](/destinations/hunza/passu-cones). Staying a night here makes Khunjerab Pass an easy day trip.

## Choosing where to stay

For a week in Hunza, we usually split the stay: a few nights in Karimabad or Duikar for the forts and the views, then a night or two in Upper Hunza. It keeps the driving short and puts you in the right place for the best light.
