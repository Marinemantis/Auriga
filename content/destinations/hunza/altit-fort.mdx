Altit Fort stands on the edge of a sheer cliff, hundreds of metres above the Hunza River. It is the oldest fort in the valley, built centuries before Baltit, and its tall watchtower once guarded the approach to the old kingdom of Hunza.

## History

Altit was the original seat of the rulers of Hunza, before the royal family moved to [Baltit Fort](/destinations/hunza/baltit-fort) above Karimabad. The fort's stone and timber walls, carved doors and small dark rooms show how life was lived here for centuries. The **Shikari tower**, the fort's watchtower, is its most striking feature.

## Restoration

By the late twentieth century the fort and the old village around it were crumbling. The Aga Khan Trust for Culture restored both, working with the people of Altit, and the project is widely seen as a model of community-led conservation. Today Altit is a living village, not a museum piece.

## The Royal Garden

Below the fort lies the **Royal Garden**, an old orchard of apricot and other fruit trees. It is a peaceful place to sit after the visit, and in spring it is full of blossom.
