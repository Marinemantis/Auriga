Beyond the turquoise expanse of Attabad Lake, the Karakoram Highway enters Gojal, the upper part of the Hunza valley. Gulmit is its largest village: a spread of fields, apricot orchards and old stone houses on a broad shelf above the river, with the Passu Cones rising to the north.

## Wakhi culture

The people of Gojal are mostly Wakhi, with their own language and traditions, distinct from the Burusho of central Hunza. In Gulmit you can see this in the architecture of old houses, in the handwoven carpets made by local women, and in the warmth of village life.

## History

Gulmit was once the summer capital of the rulers of Hunza, and a small cultural museum in the village keeps objects from that history.

## Things to do

- Walk through the old village and the orchards.
- Visit the **carpet weavers** and the village **museum**.
- Drive on to the **Hussaini suspension bridge** and **[Borith Lake](/destinations/hunza/borith-lake)**.
- See the **[Passu Cones](/destinations/hunza/passu-cones)** and walk towards the Passu and Batura glaciers.

## When to go

April to October. Autumn is especially beautiful, when colour comes to Upper Hunza first; see [Hunza in autumn](/destinations/hunza/in-autumn).
