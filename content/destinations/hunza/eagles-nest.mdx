Above the village of Altit, a road zig-zags up the hillside to Duikar, a small village on a ridge high above central Hunza. From the rocks above the village, the view opens out across the whole valley: Rakaposhi to the south, the needle of Ladyfinger and the wall of Ultar above Karimabad, and the peaks of Nagar across the river.

## Sunrise or sunset

Eagle's Nest is famous for both. At **sunrise**, the highest summits catch the first light while the valley is still in shadow, and the colour moves slowly down the mountains. It is cold and quiet, and well worth the early start. At **sunset**, warm light washes across the whole valley, and the ridge is livelier.

If you can, stay overnight at Duikar and see both.

## Getting there

The road from Altit to Duikar is steep and narrow, and takes around half an hour from Karimabad. The viewpoint is a short walk from the end of the road. In the dark before sunrise, it is worth having a driver who knows the road.

## Where to stay

The **Eagle's Nest Hotel** sits right on the ridge, so you can step outside at first light. It is where we stay on [The Realm of Fairies](/tours/realm-of-fairies), and it makes the early start effortless.

## Tips

- Bring warm layers, even in summer. The ridge is exposed and cold before dawn.
- Check the sunrise time the night before and arrive 20 minutes early.
- A telephoto lens is useful for the distant peaks; a wide lens for the valley.
