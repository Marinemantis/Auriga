Chapursan is the northernmost valley of Hunza. From the Karakoram Highway near Sost, a road follows the Chapursan River west through a string of small Wakhi villages, towards the high passes that lead into Afghanistan's Wakhan Corridor. It is remote, wide and beautiful, and very few visitors make the journey.

## The valley

The landscape is high and open: stony river flats, small green fields, and stone houses sheltered from the wind, with snow peaks on both sides. Villages such as Reshit and Zood Khun sit towards the head of the valley, where life still follows the rhythm of herding and short summers.

## Baba Ghundi

Near the head of the valley stands the shrine of **Baba Ghundi**, a revered saint, and a place of pilgrimage for people across Upper Hunza.

## When to go

May to October, when the road is open and the villages are busy with the summer. Winters here are long and severe.

## Getting there

Chapursan is reached from Sost, at the top of the Hunza valley, by a road that can be rough in places. It combines naturally with the [Khunjerab Pass](/destinations/hunza/khunjerab-pass) and, for the adventurous, [Shimshal Valley](/destinations/hunza/shimshal-valley). Rules for border areas change, so check our guide to [NOCs and permits](/travel/noc-permits).
