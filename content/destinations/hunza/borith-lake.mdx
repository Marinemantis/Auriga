Most travellers drive straight past the turning to Borith Lake on their way to Passu. That is a pity. A short, steep road from the Karakoram Highway above Hussaini leads to a still, slightly brackish lake in a wide bowl of brown mountains, with a few houses and almost no one else.

## The lake

Borith is not the turquoise of Attabad. It is quieter and greyer, and on still days it reflects the mountains around it. In spring and autumn, migrating birds rest on the water. It is a place for sitting, walking and listening to the wind.

## Walks

From the lake, a trail climbs onto the ridge above the **Passu Glacier**, with views across the ice and towards the Passu Cones. It is a good half-day walk at an easy pace. Lower down, the **Hussaini suspension bridge** over the Hunza River is a short drive away, and walking it is a highlight of many Hunza journeys.

## When to go

From April to October. In spring the fields around the lake are green; in autumn the trees turn gold. Winter is very cold, and the lake can freeze.
