Hunza is easy to rush and hard to forget. The road through the valley passes so many famous views that it is tempting to tick them off from the car window. The places below are worth stopping for properly.

## In and around Karimabad

- **[Baltit Fort](/destinations/hunza/baltit-fort)**, the old royal fort above Karimabad, now a museum.
- **Altit Fort**, the older of the two, on a cliff above the river, with the Royal Garden below.
- **Karimabad bazaar**, for Hunza caps, carpets, gemstones and dried apricots.
- **[Eagle's Nest](/destinations/hunza/eagles-nest)** at Duikar, for sunrise or sunset over the peaks of central Hunza.
- **The water channels**, centuries-old irrigation channels that make easy, beautiful walks along the hillsides.

## Along the Karakoram Highway

- **[Attabad Lake](/destinations/hunza/attabad-lake)**, the turquoise lake formed by a landslide in 2010.
- **Gulmit**, a historic village in Upper Hunza.
- **The Hussaini Suspension Bridge**, one of the most famous (and nerve-testing) footbridges in Pakistan.
- **Borith Lake**, a quiet lake above Hussaini.
- **The [Passu Cones](/destinations/hunza/passu-cones)**, the jagged spires above Passu.

## Glaciers and walks

Hunza has glaciers you can walk to in a day. The Passu and Batura glaciers lie close to the highway in Upper Hunza, and the walk up to Ultar meadow above Karimabad gives a close look at the peaks above the town. Across the river in Nagar, the Hopar Glacier is a short drive from the valley floor.

## Higher and further

- **[Khunjerab Pass](/destinations/hunza/khunjerab-pass)**, the high border crossing with China at around 4,700 metres.
- **[Shimshal](/destinations/hunza/shimshal-valley)**, a remote valley east of Passu, reached by a dramatic mountain road.

## Seasonal experiences

Spring brings [cherry blossom](/pakistan/festivals/cherry-blossom-hunza) to the orchards; autumn turns the whole valley gold. Both are worth planning a journey around.
