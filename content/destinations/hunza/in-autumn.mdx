If we could choose only one season in Hunza, many of us would choose autumn. From early October the poplars along the water channels, the apricot and cherry orchards and the terraced fields slowly turn yellow, then gold and red, and the colour spreads across the whole valley.

## When to go

Colour usually peaks in the second half of October. It begins first in Upper Hunza, around Gulmit and Passu, and moves down the valley over a few weeks. By mid-November most of the leaves have fallen and winter is close.

## Weather

Autumn brings the most settled weather of the year. Skies are clear and blue, the light is soft and golden, and the peaks often carry their first fresh snow. Days are pleasantly cool; nights turn cold, especially in November. See our [Hunza weather guide](/destinations/hunza/weather).

## What to do

- Walk the **water channels** above Karimabad and Altit, lined with golden poplars.
- See sunrise from **[Eagle's Nest](/destinations/hunza/eagles-nest)**, when the valley below is full of colour.
- Drive to **[Gulmit](/destinations/hunza/gulmit)** and **[Passu](/destinations/hunza/passu-cones)**, where colour comes first.
- Photograph **[Altit Fort](/destinations/hunza/altit-fort)** and **[Baltit Fort](/destinations/hunza/baltit-fort)** above the orchards.

## Planning

Autumn is quieter than summer, but the best hotels still fill in October, so plan ahead. See our [Hunza tours](/tours/hunza) or ask us to plan an autumn journey.
