Karimabad is the main town of central Hunza. Its lanes climb steeply up the hillside towards Baltit Fort, and almost every corner has a view: Rakaposhi across the valley, the needle of Ladyfinger and the wall of Ultar above, and terraced orchards falling away to the river.

## Baltit Fort

At the top of the town stands [Baltit Fort](/destinations/hunza/baltit-fort), the old royal residence of the Mirs of Hunza, restored and now a museum. The walk up through the old village to the fort is one of the best introductions to Hunza.

## The bazaar

Karimabad's main street is lined with small shops selling Hunza caps, handwoven carpets and shawls, gemstones, dried apricots and apricot oil. It is a good place to sit in a café with walnut cake and watch the valley.

## Water channel walks

Old irrigation channels run along the hillside above and below the town, carrying glacier water to the fields. The paths beside them make easy, beautiful walks, shaded by poplars and orchards. Above the town, a steeper trail climbs towards Ultar meadow.

## Around Karimabad

- **[Altit Fort](/destinations/hunza/altit-fort)**, the older of Hunza's two great forts, a short drive away.
- **[Eagle's Nest](/destinations/hunza/eagles-nest)** at Duikar, for sunrise over the valley.
- **[Attabad Lake](/destinations/hunza/attabad-lake)** and **[Passu](/destinations/hunza/passu-cones)**, up the Karakoram Highway.
