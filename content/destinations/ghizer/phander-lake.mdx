Most travellers heading north from Gilgit turn right, up the Karakoram Highway towards Hunza. Turn left instead, and the road follows the Gilgit River west into the Ghizer valley, past orchards and villages, towards the Shandur Pass and Chitral. Deep in the valley, the river slows and spreads into Phander Lake, a still, clear, blue-green lake ringed by willows and mountains.

## Why go

Phander's appeal is its calm. There are no big hotels or busy viewpoints, just a quiet village, clear water, trout in the river and long evenings. It is the kind of place where a short stop turns into a second night. Spelling varies on maps and signs: you will see Phander, Phunder and Phandar.

## Getting there

Phander lies on the road from Gilgit towards Shandur, a long day's drive through the Ghizer valley, usually broken with stops along the way. It is also a natural overnight halt on the journey between Gilgit and Chitral over the Shandur Pass, and on the way to Khukush Lake.

## When to go

The valley is at its best from late spring to autumn. Summer is green and mild, while autumn turns the trees along the lake gold. Winters are cold, and the road west towards Shandur closes with snow.

## Nearby

- **Khukush Lake**, a remote alpine lake reached on a trek from the valley.
- **Shandur Pass**, home of the summer polo festival.
- **Gupis** and **Yasin**, quiet valleys along the Ghizer road.
