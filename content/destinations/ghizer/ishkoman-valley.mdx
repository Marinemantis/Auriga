The Ishkoman valley branches north from the Ghizer River near **Gahkuch**. It climbs through a string of villages, including **Chatorkhand** and **Imit**, towards the high country of the Karambar Pass, close to Afghanistan's Wakhan Corridor.

## The valley

Ishkoman is green and quiet: fields and orchards in the lower valley, wilder landscapes higher up. Few visitors come here, and life follows the seasons.

## People and culture

Several communities share the valley, including Wakhi, Khowar and Shina speakers. Villages are welcoming, and the valley has strong traditions of hospitality.

## Towards Karambar

From the upper valley, trekking routes lead north to **[Karambar Lake](/trekking/karambar-lake)**, one of the highest lakes in Pakistan, on the watershed with Broghil in Chitral.

## Getting there

From Gilgit, the road follows the Gilgit River west towards Gahkuch, then turns north up the Ishkoman valley.

## Permits

The upper valley lies close to the border. Rules for foreign visitors change, so see our guide to [NOCs and permits](/travel/noc-permits).

## When to go

May to October. Autumn brings gold to the lower valley.
