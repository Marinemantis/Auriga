The Yasin valley, often spelled **Yaseen**, branches north from the Ghizer River near [Gupis](/destinations/ghizer/gupis). It is a broad, fertile valley of fields and orchards, with villages strung along the river and snow peaks rising on every side.

## The valley

Yasin's emerald fields of wheat and maize, lines of poplars and stone houses make it one of the most beautiful valleys in Ghizer. Higher up, the valley leads towards the passes of the Hindu Kush and the high pastures where herders spend the summer.

## History and culture

Yasin was once a small mountain kingdom, with a history of rivalry and alliance with its neighbours in Chitral, Gilgit and Hunza. Its people speak Burushaski and Khowar, among other languages, and are known for their hospitality. Yasin is also known across Pakistan as home to **Lalak Jan**, a soldier awarded the Nishan-e-Haider, the country's highest military honour.

## Getting there

From Gilgit, the road follows the Gilgit and Ghizer rivers west to Gupis, then turns north into the Yasin valley.

## When to go

May to October. Summer brings green fields; autumn turns the valley gold.
