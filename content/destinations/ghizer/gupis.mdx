Gupis is a small town in the Ghizer valley, on the road west from Gilgit towards Phander and the Shandur Pass. It sits where the Yasin valley opens to the main river, and it has long been a crossroads.

## Gupis Fort

The old **Gupis Fort** stands as a silent guardian of ancient trade routes, a reminder of the valley's strategic past. On our [Wilderness of Chitral & Phunder](/tours/chitral-phunder) journey, we explore it on the way from Yasin.

## Khalti Lake

Near Gupis, **Khalti Lake** is a long, still, turquoise lake beside the road, fringed with trees. It is a peaceful place to stop, and in the cool early morning it reflects the mountains perfectly. Our [Khukush Lake & Phunder](/tours/khukush-lake-phunder) journey begins with a gentle day at Khalti Lake, to acclimatise.

## The road west

Beyond Gupis, the road continues to **[Phander](/destinations/ghizer/phander-lake)** and its lake, and on over the **Shandur Pass** to Chitral. North of Gupis lies the **[Yasin valley](/destinations/ghizer/yasin-valley)**.

## When to go

May to October. Autumn is especially beautiful in Ghizer, when the poplars and orchards turn gold.
