[Fairy Meadows](/destinations/fairy-meadows) lies at around 3,300 metres, and its season is short. Here is how the months compare.

## May: opening

The season begins as the snow melts and the track opens. Snow often lingers in the forest and on the higher trails, and nights are cold. The meadow is quiet.

## June: early summer

One of the best months. The meadow turns green, the trail towards Beyal Camp opens, and the weather is often settled, before the summer crowds.

## July and August: high summer

The meadow is at its greenest, with wildflowers in the clearings. These are the busiest months, especially during Eid and the school holidays, and afternoon cloud can hide the summit.

## September and October: autumn

Clear, crisp air and the sharpest views of Nanga Parbat. The crowds thin, and by October the trees begin to turn. Nights are cold. The season ends as snow returns.

## Winter

From around November, snow closes the track and the meadow. It is not a winter destination.

## What we recommend

June or September, for open trails, settled weather and fewer people. On [The Realm of Fairies](/tours/realm-of-fairies), we stay two nights at our camp on the meadow. See also [how to reach Fairy Meadows](/destinations/fairy-meadows/how-to-reach).
