[Fairy Meadows](/destinations/fairy-meadows) lies at around 3,300 metres beneath the Raikot Face of Nanga Parbat. Getting there takes three stages: a drive, a jeep ride and a walk.

## 1. To Raikot Bridge

**Raikot Bridge** is on the Karakoram Highway, south of Gilgit. From Gilgit it is a drive of around two hours. From Islamabad, it is a long road journey; see [Islamabad to Hunza](/travel/islamabad-to-hunza) for the routes north.

## 2. The jeep track

At Raikot Bridge, private vehicles stop and local jeeps take over. The track climbs steeply up the side of the gorge towards the village of **Tato**: narrow, unpaved and high above the river. It is one of the most famous jeep rides in Pakistan. Local drivers know every bend.

## 3. The walk

From the end of the track, the last stretch is on foot: a walk of around **three hours** through pine forest and clearings, climbing gently, until the meadow opens out with Nanga Parbat filling the sky. Ponies are usually available, and porters carry luggage.

## Tips

- Travel light, and pack an overnight bag separately.
- Start early, to walk in the cool of the morning.
- Bring a warm layer: the meadow is far cooler than the Karakoram Highway.

## When to go

Fairy Meadows is accessible from around May to October. See [the best time to visit Fairy Meadows](/destinations/fairy-meadows/best-time-to-visit).
