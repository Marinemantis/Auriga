From Astore town, a road climbs steeply through some of the finest pine forest in Gilgit-Baltistan to **Rama Meadows**: wide, green, and ringed by snow peaks. Beyond the meadows, a path continues up to **Rama Lake**, an alpine lake beneath the snows of the Nanga Parbat massif.

## Rama Meadows

The meadows are a place for walks among the pines, picnics beside the stream, and long views across the valley. In summer, herders graze their animals here.

## Rama Lake

The lake lies higher, beneath glaciers and ice walls. It is reached by a walk from the meadows, or partly by jeep when the track allows. On a still morning, it mirrors the mountains above.

## When to go

June to September. Early summer brings fresh green; autumn brings clear skies and colour to the forest. In winter, the area is under snow.
