South of Astore, the road climbs over the **Burzil Pass** and descends into a high country of rolling grassland, streams and wildflowers. This is **Minimarg**, a remote valley near the Line of Control.

## The meadows

Minimarg is known for its vast open plains and, in high summer, its fields of wildflowers. Snow peaks rise on every side. A small lake nearby, often called **Rainbow Lake**, adds to the beauty.

## Getting there

From Astore, the road climbs past Chilim and over the Burzil Pass. The journey is long and slow, and the last part is rough. Because the area lies near the Line of Control, access is controlled and permission is usually needed. See our guide to [NOCs and permits](/travel/noc-permits); we arrange this for guests.

## A night at Minimarg

On the [Seven Day Himalayan Dream](/tours/himalayan-dream), guests spend a night at **Minimarg High Camp**, our private camp on the plateau, with canvas tents, proper beds, and a sky full of stars. See [camping in Gilgit-Baltistan](/experiences/camping).

## When to go

June to September, when the pass is open and the meadows are green. Wildflowers peak in July.

## Combine with

[Rama Lake](/destinations/astore/rama-lake) and the [Rupal Face](/trekking/rupal-face) of Nanga Parbat, as on the Himalayan Dream. See our [Astore guide](/destinations/astore).
