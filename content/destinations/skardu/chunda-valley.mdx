Around Skardu, the main valley is wide, dry and dramatic. Folded into the mountains on either side are small green valleys, watered by glacier streams, where villages, orchards and terraced fields climb the slopes. Chunda is one of them.

## The valley

Chunda is a place of quiet village life: stone houses, apricot and apple trees, channels of cold water running beside the fields, and mountains rising steeply behind. It sees few visitors, which is much of its charm.

## When to go

From April to October. Spring brings blossom, summer brings green fields and ripening fruit, and autumn turns the orchards gold.

## Visiting Chunda

Chunda is best visited as part of a day exploring the valleys around Skardu, alongside places such as the [Kachura lakes](/destinations/skardu/kachura-lake) and [Basho Valley](/destinations/skardu/basho-valley).
