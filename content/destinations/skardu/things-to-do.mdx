Skardu sits in a wide valley on the Indus, surrounded by mountains, at the heart of Baltistan. Few places in Pakistan offer so much variety so close together: you can walk on sand dunes in the morning, eat lunch beside an alpine lake and watch the sun set from a sixteenth-century fort.

## Lakes

- **Lower Kachura Lake and [Shangrila Resort](/destinations/skardu/shangrila-resort)**, the classic Skardu view.
- **Upper Kachura Lake**, deeper, clearer and quieter.
- **Satpara Lake**, above Skardu on the road to Deosai.
- **Sheosar Lake**, high on the Deosai Plains.

## Forts and palaces

- **[Kharpocho Fort](/destinations/skardu/kharpocho-fort)**, on its ridge above town, with the best view of the valley.
- **[Shigar Fort](/destinations/skardu/shigar-fort)**, the restored royal palace in the Shigar valley.
- **[Khaplu Palace](/destinations/skardu/khaplu)**, further east in the Ghanche valley.

## Deserts

- **The [Katpana cold desert](/destinations/skardu/katpana-desert)**, near the airport.
- **The Sarfaranga cold desert**, on the road to Shigar.

## Deosai and the high country

- **The [Deosai Plains](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains)**, one of the highest plateaus in the world, open in summer.
- **Manthokha Waterfall**, a popular day trip east of Skardu.
- **Basho Valley**, a green side valley of forests and meadows.

## Gateway to K2

Skardu is the starting point for expeditions and treks into the Baltoro region, towards Concordia and K2 Base Camp, via Shigar and Askole.
