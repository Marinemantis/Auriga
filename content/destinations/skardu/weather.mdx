Skardu sits at around 2,230 metres in a wide valley where the Shigar River meets the Indus. High mountains block most of the monsoon, so it is very dry: summers are warm and sunny, winters cold and clear, and spring brings what little rain and snow the valley gets.

## Spring: March to May

Days warm quickly, from cool in March to pleasant in May, while nights stay chilly. Blossom comes to the orchards of Skardu and Shigar in April. This is the wettest part of the year, though still dry by most standards.

## Summer: June to August

Warm, sunny and very dry, with afternoons around 30°C in the valley. This is the season for the high country: the [Deosai Plains](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains) are open and green, and treks into the Baltoro begin. Nights on Deosai can still fall close to freezing.

## Autumn: September to November

Clear skies and settled weather, with poplars and orchards turning gold in October. Nights get cold by November. One of the best times to visit.

## Winter: December to February

Cold and quiet, with average highs only a few degrees above freezing and hard frosts at night. Deosai is closed, and flights are more often affected by weather.

## Altitude matters

Temperatures drop by roughly six or seven degrees for every thousand metres you climb. On the Deosai Plains, at around 4,100 metres, it is far colder than in Skardu town, even on a summer's day.
