A short drive from Skardu, the Kachura lakes are among the best-loved sights in Baltistan. There are two, and they could not be more different.

## Upper Kachura Lake

Upper Kachura is a deep, clear lake in a steep bowl of mountains, its water a remarkable shade of blue-green. It is reached by a short walk down from the road through the village. On a calm morning, with the mountains reflected in the water, it is one of the most peaceful places in the north. Boats can take you out across the lake.

## Lower Kachura Lake

Lower Kachura is smaller and gentler, ringed by gardens and orchards. It is best known as the setting of **[Shangrila Resort](/destinations/skardu/shangrila-resort)**, with its red-roofed chalets and famous aircraft café.

## When to go

April to October. Spring brings blossom to the orchards around the lakes; autumn brings gold. In winter the lakes can freeze, and the area is very quiet.
