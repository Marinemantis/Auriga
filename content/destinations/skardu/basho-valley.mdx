Most of the Skardu valley is dry, brown and dramatic. Basho is the exception. A jeep track leaves the Indus valley west of Skardu and climbs into a narrow side valley, and within an hour the landscape changes completely: pine forests, wide green meadows, clear streams and snow peaks above.

## The valley

The lower valley is dotted with small villages and fields. Higher up, the forest thickens and opens into meadows where shepherds graze their flocks in summer. It is a place for walking, picnicking beside the stream, and simply enjoying the green after the desert.

## Getting there

The track up Basho is rough, steep in places and best tackled in a sturdy vehicle with an experienced local driver. Allow around one and a half to two hours from Skardu.

## When to go

From May to October. In early summer the meadows are at their greenest; in autumn the trees turn gold. In winter the track is usually snowbound.

## Visiting Basho

Basho makes a fine day trip from Skardu, and is a good contrast to the lakes and deserts around the town. It is part of our [Where Mountains Meet the Stars](/tours/mountains-meet-stars) journey. See all our [things to do in Skardu](/destinations/skardu/things-to-do).
