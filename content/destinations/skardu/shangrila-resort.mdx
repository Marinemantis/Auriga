A few kilometres from Skardu, the road drops to a small, still lake ringed by orchards and mountains. Around its shore, red-roofed chalets, pagoda-style buildings and manicured gardens make up Shangrila Resort, often described as "heaven on earth" and one of the most famous sights in Baltistan.

## What it's like

Shangrila is set around **Lower Kachura Lake**, with paths through the gardens and along the water, and views of the mountains reflected in the lake on calm mornings. One of its best-known features is the fuselage of an old aircraft, brought to the grounds and turned into a café. It is popular, especially in summer, and the gardens can be busy with day visitors.

## Visiting

Even if you stay elsewhere, Shangrila is worth a stop for a walk around the lake, ideally early in the morning or late in the afternoon when it is quieter and the light is softest. **Upper Kachura Lake**, a short drive further on, is deeper, clearer and much more peaceful, and well worth seeing too.

## Shangrila or somewhere quieter?

Shangrila suits travellers who want a classic resort by the water within easy reach of Skardu. If you would rather have somewhere quieter and steeped in history, the **Shigar Fort Residence**, a restored seventeenth-century royal fort in the Shigar valley, is where we base our Skardu journeys. Many of our guests still visit Shangrila and the Kachura lakes as a morning out.

## When to go

Spring brings blossom to the orchards around the lake, summer is the busiest season, and autumn brings golden colour and fewer visitors. Winters are cold, and the lake can freeze.
