Satpara Lake lies in a bowl of bare brown mountains just south of Skardu, on the road to the Deosai Plains. Its water comes down from the plateau above, and on a still day the lake is a deep, startling blue against the dry slopes.

## The lake and the dam

Satpara is a natural lake, enlarged by a dam at its lower end. The dam supplies much of Skardu's water and electricity. The road from Skardu climbs past the dam and runs along the lake shore, with wide views across the water.

## Manthal Buddha rock

On the way up from Skardu, near the village of Manthal, a large boulder carries a carved image of the Buddha, a reminder of Baltistan's Buddhist past. It is a short detour and well worth a stop.

## The road to Deosai

Beyond the lake, the road keeps climbing towards the [Deosai Plains](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains). Satpara is the natural place to pause on the way up, or to stop for tea on the way back down. On [Where Mountains Meet the Stars](/tours/mountains-meet-stars), we cross Deosai and descend into Skardu along this road.

## When to go

April to October. The road beyond Satpara to Deosai is usually open only from around June to September.
