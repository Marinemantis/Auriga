Sheosar Lake lies near the western edge of the [Deosai Plains](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains), at around 4,100 metres. On a calm day it is a sheet of blue mirroring the snow peaks around the plateau; when the wind rises, it turns dark and wild. Either way, the scale of the sky above it is unforgettable.

## The lake

Sheosar is a wide, open lake, fed by snowmelt and surrounded by the rolling grasslands of Deosai. In July and August, wildflowers bloom along its shores. On clear days, the distant summit of Nanga Parbat can be seen from parts of the plateau.

## Getting there

The road across Deosai links Skardu, via [Satpara Lake](/destinations/skardu/satpara-lake), with Chilim and [Astore](/destinations/astore). Sheosar sits towards the Astore end of the crossing. It is a long, rough day across the plateau in a sturdy vehicle.

## When to go

Only in summer. The road across Deosai is usually open from around June to September, depending on the year's snow. Even in midsummer, nights here are close to freezing.

## Seeing Sheosar on a journey

On [Where Mountains Meet the Stars](/tours/mountains-meet-stars), we cross Deosai from Astore towards Skardu and spend a night at our glamping camp on the plateau, at 4,114 metres. See also our guide to [stargazing in Gilgit-Baltistan](/experiences/stargazing).

## What to bring

Warm layers, a hat and gloves, sun protection and sunglasses. The sun is fierce at this altitude, and the wind can be bitter.
