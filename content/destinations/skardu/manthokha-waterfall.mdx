Manthokha is a tall, narrow waterfall in the village of the same name, in the Kharmang valley east of Skardu. The water falls through green trees into a pool at the foot of the cliff, and the spray keeps the air cool even on the hottest summer days.

## The waterfall

The falls drop in a long white ribbon from the cliff above the village. Below them, shady orchards and streams make a natural place to rest, and simple stalls nearby serve tea and food in season. It is busiest in summer, with families from Skardu and beyond.

## Getting there

The road from Skardu follows the Indus east into Kharmang, through a deep, dramatic gorge. Allow around two to three hours each way, which makes Manthokha a full day out.

## When to go

From May to September, when the falls are full with snowmelt and the valley is green. In autumn the flow drops; in winter the falls can partly freeze.
