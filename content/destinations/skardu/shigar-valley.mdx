Cross the Indus north of Skardu and the road enters the Shigar valley. After the sand dunes of the Sarfaranga cold desert, the valley turns green: apricot and apple orchards, poplars, fields of wheat and barley, and old villages of stone and timber, all beneath bare, towering mountains.

## A royal valley

Shigar was once the seat of the Amacha rulers, one of the old royal houses of Baltistan. Their palace, **[Shigar Fort](/destinations/skardu/shigar-fort)**, has been restored by the Aga Khan Trust for Culture and is now the Shigar Fort Residence, a heritage hotel and museum.

## Amburiq Mosque

In the village stands the **Amburiq Mosque**, one of the oldest in Baltistan, built in a style that shows the region's links with Kashmir and Tibet. It too has been carefully restored.

## Sarfaranga cold desert

On the way from Skardu, the road crosses the **Sarfaranga cold desert**, a stretch of high-altitude sand dunes. See our guide to the [Katpana and Sarfaranga cold deserts](/destinations/skardu/katpana-desert).

## The road to K2

Beyond the village of Shigar, a road follows the valley north towards Askole, the last village before the Baltoro Glacier and the starting point for treks to Concordia and K2 Base Camp.

## Staying in Shigar

We base guests at the Shigar Fort Residence on [The Last Paradise](/tours/last-paradise), [Where Mountains Meet the Stars](/tours/mountains-meet-stars) and the [Nagma Valley Trek](/tours/nagma-valley). Waking up in the valley, with the orchards and the river around you, is one of the great pleasures of Baltistan.
