Skardu town is a busy market town and the gateway to Baltistan. The most memorable places to stay, though, are in the valleys around it: restored royal buildings in Shigar and Khaplu, resorts beside the Kachura lakes, and camps high on the Deosai Plains.

## Shigar Fort Residence

A 400-year-old royal fort in the Shigar valley, restored and converted into Pakistan's most atmospheric heritage hotel. Every room is different, the gardens are full of fruit trees, and the sound of the Shigar River carries through the courtyards. It is our base on [The Last Paradise](/tours/last-paradise), [Where Mountains Meet the Stars](/tours/mountains-meet-stars) and the [Nagma Valley Trek](/tours/nagma-valley). See our [Shigar Fort guide](/destinations/skardu/shigar-fort).

## Khaplu Palace

Further east, the restored palace of the Rajas of Khaplu is now a heritage hotel too, set among gardens above the Shyok valley. It is a beautiful place to spend a night on the way to the Ghanche valleys. See our [Khaplu guide](/destinations/skardu/khaplu).

## Lakeside at Kachura

The Kachura lakes, around half an hour from Skardu, have several lakeside hotels and resorts, including the well-known Shangrila Resort on Lower Kachura. See [Shangrila Resort](/destinations/skardu/shangrila-resort) and [Kachura Lake](/destinations/skardu/kachura-lake).

## Deosai Plains Camp

For one night under the darkest skies in Pakistan, our signature glamping camp on the [Deosai Plains](/destinations/skardu/deosai-plains) has canvas tents at 4,114 metres, proper beds and gas heaters.

We choose stays for their setting, comfort and character, and we know every one personally. See our [Skardu tours](/tours/skardu) or ask us to plan a journey around the places you would like to stay.
